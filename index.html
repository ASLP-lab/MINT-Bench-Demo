Overview

MINT-Bench evaluates instruction-following TTS with a hierarchical multi-axis taxonomy, a scalable data construction pipeline, and a hybrid evaluation protocol.

1. Taxonomy

Easy / Hard / Special, with timbre, style, composition, and extra-vocal control.

2. Data Pipeline

Node specification, structured label planning, and instruction-text realization.

3. Evaluation

Progressive assessment of content consistency, instruction following, and perceptual quality.
